Leading Joshua Campaign since 2007

Abbi & Per Åkvist

Abbi and Per Åkvist lead Joshua Campaign Ethiopia and the Sweden office. They came to Ethiopia in 2005 and joined Joshua Campaign two years later. Their shared vision is direct: reach the unreached and help the helpless.

Per is from Borås. A former biker, he encountered God in Sweden’s faith movement in the mid-1980s and began a life of pioneer ministry. Abbi was born in Ethiopia and came to Sweden at fifteen after a childhood marked by poverty and hard work. Adopted into a Swedish family, she describes being healed from hatred and bitterness through God’s love and receiving a lasting call to bring the Gospel and practical help to poor women and children in her first homeland.

“A human right for every person to hear the Gospel at least once in their lifetime.”

Their leadership keeps Joshua Campaign close to the local church: campaigns mobilize pastors, new believers are connected for follow-up, and pioneer leaders are equipped for sustained ministry.

Founder · Founding vision

Karl Härgestam

Karl Härgestam founded Joshua Campaign International in 1997. He left Sweden at 21 with a mandate to take the Gospel to unreached peoples in Africa, and together with Jennifer Härgestam helped establish a movement marked by pioneer evangelism, large-scale outreach, aviation and church planting.

Today Karl serves as Global Missions Director for the Pentecostal Church of God. His enduring message—One Chance for Every Person—connects the founding impulse of Joshua Campaign with a wider global call to give every person one chance to hear the Gospel.
