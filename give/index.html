Your part in the assignment

Pray. Go. Give.

Stand with Joshua Campaign as local churches prepare new campaigns, pioneer leaders carry the Gospel forward, and unreached communities receive a clear chance to hear. The aim is to give every person one chance to hear the Gospel.

Joshua Campaign was founded in Ethiopia in 1997. The public record of the work is 4.2 million decisions, while about 3.37 billion people remain unreached. Gifts help local churches prepare campaigns, follow up with those who respond, and support pioneer pastors.

What gifts support

  • A campaign is normally planned six months ahead, including two months of active preparation, and costs approximately SEK 250,000.
  • A pioneer pastor can be supported through the Swedish office at SEK 600 per month for two years.
  • Supporters may give SEK 600 or SEK 200 per month for at least two years, choose another monthly amount, or make a one-time gift.
