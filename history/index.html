The Joshua Campaign archive

A rich history.

These older photographs preserve the story behind today’s work: Karl and Per serving together, mass campaigns taking shape, teams forming, and field tools changing as the mission reached farther. Joshua Campaign was founded in 1997.

Mass campaigns through the years

From daylight fields to evening crusades, the scale changed from place to place while the purpose remained constant: preach Jesus clearly and connect people with local churches.

Tools that changed with the mission

Sound-stage trucks, tents and aviation made open-air campaigns and remote access possible. The equipment evolved over the years; the field purpose stayed the same.

The sound stage on the move

Different generations of mobile trucks carried sound, staging and campaign identity from one field to the next.

Reaching beyond the roads

Helicopter access helped teams connect with remote communities and support practical field needs.

The people and the preparation

Campaigns depended on teams, training and temporary field infrastructure long before the first public meeting began.
