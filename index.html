The work on the ground

Gospel proclamation that leads somewhere.

Jesus Christ remains at the center. Campaigns are prepared with local pastors, followed by discipleship, and connected to the long work of healthy local churches.

01

Mass evangelism

Five-day campaigns proclaim Jesus as Lord. Local congregations prepare, host and follow up with those who respond.

02

Church planting

Ethiopian leaders establish congregations where the Gospel has not had a strong presence, building local capacity beyond a single event.

03

Pioneer mission

Mission aviation and field teams extend access to remote communities, with practical help where roads cannot.

About Joshua Campaign

From pioneer aviation to a field-led movement.

Karl and Jennifer Härgestam founded Joshua Campaign in Ethiopia in 1997. The ministry now has offices in Ethiopia, Sweden and California. Since then the work has recorded 4.2 million decisions, while about 3.37 billion people remain unreached.

Distinct identity. Shared assignment.

Joshua Campaign keeps its own story, leadership and field identity while standing in clear alignment with the One Chance conviction: give every person one chance to hear the Gospel. Operation 326 puts that conviction to work by identifying frontier gaps and helping prayer, pioneers and resources move toward them.
