About Joshua Campaign

From pioneer aviation to a field-led movement.

Karl and Jennifer Härgestam founded Joshua Campaign in Ethiopia in 1997. Karl, raised in Storuman in Sweden’s Pentecostal tradition, began mission work in Tanzania in the late 1980s. A call to reach unreached peoples took him to California for helicopter training, where he met Jennifer. They moved to Ethiopia in the early 1990s, and Karl flew with the Swiss organization Heli-Mission before launching Joshua Campaign.

Today the ministry has offices in Ethiopia, Sweden and California, with partners around the world, including the Evangelical Churches Fellowship (ECFE) for campaigns. Since 1997, the work has recorded 4.2 million decisions. An estimated 3.37 billion people remain unreached. Local churches carry the work forward.

The Joshua Campaign way

Keep the roots. Strengthen the reach.

Three commitments shape the work: proclaim Jesus through mass evangelism, mobilize local churches to reach beyond their walls, and build mission networks across churches, denominations, ministries and movements around the world.

“Go through the camp and tell the people, ‘Get your provisions ready. Three days from now you will cross the Jordan here to go in and take possession of the land the Lord your God is giving you for your own.’” — Joshua 1:11

Campaigns are not the finish line. They serve the local church’s long obedience—disciples formed, leaders raised and congregations planted where the Gospel needs a durable home.
